How this works

Every game in the training data is represented only by information known before tip-off: each team's season and last-10-game win percentage and scoring margin, home/away-specific record, days of rest and back-to-back status, strength of schedule faced so far, current win/loss streak, and head-to-head history against this specific opponent this season — all computed strictly from games prior to the one being predicted, to avoid leaking the outcome into the features. A random forest classifier predicts the winner; two random forest regressors predict each team's final point total. The model is evaluated on a chronological hold-out (the most recent ~20% of games by date), not a random shuffle, since shuffling would let the model train on future games to predict the past.

Scope and limitations: data was collected from basketball-reference.com game logs for the 2023-24, 2024-25, and 2025-26 regular seasons (2,268 of ~3,690 possible games — a handful of monthly schedule pages failed to load and were excluded rather than filled in with guessed data). "Current form" for each matchup uses each team's most recent rolling stats as of their last played game, not a live in-season update. Predictions reflect team-level scoring and win-rate patterns only — they do not account for injuries, trades, or lineup changes.
